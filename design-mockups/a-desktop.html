What moves & why

Same app, same pages – the navigation is reorganised so it keeps working as we add features.

1
The Admin dropdown is gone. Every admin page gets its own labelled group in the sidebar.
Admin: Overview · Events · Crew · Check-in · Emails · Support · Live Setup · Diagnostics
2
Top tabs split into groups. Main · Production (Stage, Live Show) · Club (Parents, Tools).
No more horizontal scroll from a growing tab row.
3
Command palette (new). Search / jump to any page with ⌘K, so nothing depends on finding a tab.
Replaces the "collapse tabs to icons" preference.
4
Identity pinned bottom-left. Name, role & Settings live in the sidebar footer, out of the way.
Accent / layout preferences move under Settings.
5
Live states are visible. Check-in shows a live dot; Support shows an unread badge.
The old presence banner becomes a compact status chip in the top bar.
6
Home is role-aware. Same place for everyone; staff/actors see Roster or Stage instead – unchanged behaviour.
