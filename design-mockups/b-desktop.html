What moves & why

Keeps the tab-bar feel, but groups tabs and hides the rest one level down.

1
Tabs become grouped segments. Home · Check-in · Calendar · Live stay visible.
Everything else folds into "More".
2
Admin is a labelled mega-menu from the top bar instead of the current dropdown.
Same pages, clearer heading, wider targets.
3
"More" holds Production & Club (Stage Layout, Live Show, Parents, Tools, Settings).
4
⌘K search drives everything. The real navigation is the palette; tabs are shortcuts.
5
Full-width content. No sidebar column, so wide tables (Crew, Emails, Support) fit better.
6
Trade-off: busiest of the three at the top; best when most browsing happens on big screens.
