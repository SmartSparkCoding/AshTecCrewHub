What moves & why

Navigation becomes an icon rail; the landing page becomes a live overview.

1
Icons-only rail keeps nav to ~70px and never scrolls, however many pages we add.
Hover shows a label; click expands a flyout group.
2
Home becomes an overview board. Today's schedule, check-in, support queue, response progress & quick actions at a glance.
3
Admin items are rail icons too (Crew, Overview), revealed in the flyout for admins.
4
Best on show nights – a control-room feel. Weaker for casual browsing where labels help.
