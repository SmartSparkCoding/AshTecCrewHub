# AshTec Crew Hub

The Ashford School Tech Crew platform for optional events such as The Addams Family (TAF) and High School Musical (HSM).

- **Crew members** sign in with their school email and get a one-time code. They can also edit their own first and last name, year, school email and top two preferred roles from **My Profile**; everything else is admin-managed, and editing is locked while previewing somebody else. Year is limited to the non-Staff years: picking Staff is the admin-only staff access level, so it cannot be self-assigned, and the server rejects it independently of the UI. Changing your school email keeps the old address working as a backup so a typo can't lock you out. Each show asks them Yes/No/Maybe. If they say yes, they then confirm which rehearsals and performances they'll attend. Saying "can't go" needs a reason of at least 8 words. Anyone who says No to a show is automatically marked "Not Attending Event" for everything in it. Anyone attending no performances is automatically dropped from rehearsals. Crew members also pick their top two preferred roles.
- **Admins** manage shows and their rehearsals and performances (type, kind, date or TBC, meet time, timings, things to bring, importance, and response deadlines or "unknown"). They can see expected attendance and manage the crew list: roles, heads of area, member type (Teacher/Expert/Normal Member), admin access, and notes. They can also record responses given in person.
- Roles: Stage Left, Stage Right, Microphone Management, Lighting, Sound.
- **Actors** don't sign up for events. They only see the stage layout, where they can find and email the head of each area.
- Admins can send reminder emails and messages; group messages show as one collapsible entry in the email log. Every automated email carries the same footer explaining it was sent automatically.
- **Calendar** (`/calendar`) is open to every member, not just admins: a shared month grid of every rehearsal and performance across all shows, so people can spot clashes. Filters for show and type, plus a hidden-events toggle that only admins get because hidden events are never sent to anyone else. Events without a confirmed date are listed underneath the grid rather than dropped, since a month view cannot place them.
- **Hidden shows and events** stay fully in the admin area but are removed from what members see, including their forms, reminders and stage layout. Hidden items are marked with a crossed-out eye wherever an admin sees them.
- **Crew filters**: the Crew page filters by search, year, member type, role (assigned or head of), and flags for admins, maintainers, preview accounts, staff and outstanding forms. The result count updates live and one button clears everything.
- **Help & feedback** (bottom-left button): bug reports go to chosen **maintainers**; feature requests and general support go to all admins who aren't staff/teachers. Admins track everything in the Support tab.
- **Refer to opencode**: a flag on bug reports and feature requests that marks a ticket as coding work. It is deliberately inert: setting it sends no email, schedules no job and notifies nobody. It only makes the ticket easy to find later. It shows as a badge on the Support list and the ticket page, and the list can filter to just the flagged ones. General support tickets cannot carry it, since they are questions rather than pieces of work.
- **Support tickets** get a full thread at `/admin/support/:ticketId`: public replies to the member, private notes the member never sees, status, assignee, a shared scratchpad and deletion. Maintainers are alerted again after 12 hours of silence and every 12 hours after that until somebody replies. Replies land in the replying maintainer's inbox with a warning that direct email replies are not monitored, so the ticket is the record. There is no inbound email ingestion: transfer anything useful from the inbox into a note.
- **Preview accounts**: pretend people admins can create and "preview as" to see the app from a member's view. They can't sign in or get emails.

- **Navigation**: member pages sit in the top bar, and every admin page is grouped behind a single **Admin** dropdown (Manage Events, Crew, Emails, Support) so the bar stays short. The trigger shows how many support tickets are waiting on you.
- **Guided tour**: a first-run spotlight tour greets each member once. It dims the page and punches a hole around one real control at a time, and navigation steps wait for an actual click. It only ever clicks read-only navigation, never anything that writes, so it cannot save a profile or submit a response on someone's behalf. Content is filtered by role, so an Actor is never shown show-response buttons they do not have. Progress is stored per member in the browser, so the tour runs once per device rather than once per account, and the `?` button in the header reopens it at any time.
- **Push notifications** can be tuned per kind from Settings: a member turns off just the reminders they do not want (form due, check-in prompts, overdue returns, event changes, cue calls, cover requests) and the switch follows their account to every device. Operational alerts to admins and maintainers are governed separately. Settings also lists the member's last 30 notifications so anything missed while offline can be caught up, and offers a one-click download of everything the hub holds about them (profile, show responses, attendance, tickets, availability, rota sign-ups).
- **Accessibility and compact views**: Settings adds a text-size choice (default/large/larger) and a high-contrast toggle, both per device, alongside the existing accent and density options. A next-call **widget** (`/widget`) and a bare, large-type **watch companion** (`/watch`) cover a show day on a phone home screen or a second screen, and each show has a printable **call sheet** (`/show/:id/sheet`) listing every event, its times and the crew called to it.
- **Offline and keyboard**: the app shell is already served offline by the service worker; the read-only pages a show day depends on now also fall back to their last good data when the network is gone, with a quiet banner saying so. Keyboard users can press `g` then a letter to jump to a page and `?` to see the list of shortcuts (ignored while typing).
- **Announcements** (`/announcements`): a crew-wide noticeboard every member can read, which admins post to, edit, pin and delete. Pinned notices float to the top; posting a new one fires a push to the crew, while editing or re-pinning stays quiet so a typo fix never re-pings everyone.
- **Messages** (`/messages`): private one-to-one messages between crew members, with a conversation list, unread counts and a picker for starting a new thread. A push deep-links straight to the right conversation; opening a thread marks it read. Messages are scoped to the two people in them and cannot be messaged to or from preview accounts or yourself.
- **Skills directory** (`/skills`): members tag what they can teach and what they want to learn (comma-separated, tidied and de-duplicated on save, and editable from Settings). The page is searchable and filterable by skill, answering "who can teach me lighting?" without asking around.
- **The crew cat**: the cat from the feedback forms now roams the whole app as a companion that now and then says something, and can be hidden per device. It has a page of its own at `/mascot` with its name (Tabby), a backstory and a character sheet, plus a switch to turn the companion on or off.
- **Sound effects**: a few small synthesised noises (nothing is downloaded; they are built with the Web Audio API) on deliberate actions like sending a message or posting an announcement. Muted with one switch in Settings, per device, with a preview button.
- **Show memories** (`/memories`): a wall for finished runs, gathering each show's backstage photos and the best lines from its debrief into one place, freshest run first. Photos open full size on click.
- **Home** (`/`): one landing page for every signed-in member, whatever their role. It greets you, counts down to the next performance, lists what is coming up and what still needs your answer, shows the latest announcements, your open support tickets and your badges, and offers quick links to the pages you use most. The same page grows with the role: staff and admins also get the team roster shortcut and, for admins, the queue of tickets waiting on them and one-tap links into the admin tools. Students' own event page moved to `/my-events`, and the team roster to `/team` (`/roster` still works). Actors keep their stage board one tap away at `/stage`.
- **Pinned support tickets**: admins can pin a ticket so it floats to the top of the Support list and shows a pin badge, regardless of the sort. Pinning changes nothing else: it sends no email and does not move the ticket through the workflow.
- **Manual badge grants**: alongside the badges a member earns from their attendance, admins can award a badge by hand from a member's profile, for work the app cannot see (e.g. contributions from before the hub existed). A manual grant is additive, shows on the leaderboard, profile and My Badges like any other, and can be revoked just as easily.
- **Error alerts**: when something crashes for a member, only the maintainers and the person it happened to are told, so an error reaches the people who can fix it and the person who hit it, and nobody else.
